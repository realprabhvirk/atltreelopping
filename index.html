Our story

A chainsaw, a family trade and 1985

In 1985, Ali arrived in Australia with little more than a chainsaw and tree knowledge passed down through generations of a family tradition in Fiji.

Ali started cutting trees around Logan, and the business was called Ali's Tree Lopping. That is where the "ATL" comes from.

It is still a family crew with the same work ethic: turn up on time, do the job properly, and leave the yard tidy.
