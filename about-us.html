About us

A Story That Begins in 1985

We are a family that has lived and breathed tree care since 1985, and we have been cutting trees in Slacks Creek, Springwood and Logan for most of that time. This is how it started.

Since 1985

From Fiji to Logan

In 1985, Ali arrived in Australia with little more than a chainsaw. What Ali did bring was tree knowledge, passed down through generations of a family tradition in Fiji.

That knowledge became a business: Ali's Tree Lopping. Today we go by ATL, and the letters still stand for the same name and the same family.

The island work ethic

The way we work comes from the islands. Show up, get stuck in, and get it done. That can-do spirit is behind every job, whether it is one branch or a whole yard of trees.

We arrive on time, fully equipped, and ready to restore your outdoor space. When we leave, the area is tidy.

Looking after Slacks Creek, Springwood and Logan

This is home. We are committed to sustainable practices and to protecting the local environment, and to a greener future for the community we work in.

Trees we take down are chipped and mulched on-site, and our firewood is sustainably sourced hardwood.
